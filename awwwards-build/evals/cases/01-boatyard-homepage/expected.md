# Case 01 expected

A full one-pass build from a brief that tempts habit ("add the usual sections", "maybe some 3D").

## Must

- Open by stating that `voice.md` is absent and that the run continues without one.
- Write the page task and one argument sentence, naming both next actions (commission enquiry, Saturday yard visit).
- Name the dominant behaviour as persuasion through a sequence, with browsing of finished boats as a secondary behaviour.
- Produce a content and evidence inventory that uses only the supplied material: the logbook count of 41 boats since 2009, the build photos, the finished boats, the yard photos, the hull-turning video, the lines plans, the two restoration stories.
- Mark testimonials as a dependency, not a section.
- Write each beat as concern, answer and evidence, with the reason it follows the previous beat.
- Say it explored more than one structure and give one line on why the alternative lost; present only the chosen one.
- Fill a section brief for every section, with all eleven fields.
- Tie at least one composition to a physical fact of the material, for example the lines plans as a stable drawing beside a changing photograph, or the hull-turning video as the scale or process evidence.
- Put the yard visit invitation after the material that makes a visit worth taking, with the practical details needed to act.
- Draw desktop and narrow wireframes with real labels and real image shapes, including the hero and its successor together.
- Give motion state notes (entry, active, exit, trigger, what stays readable, reduced-motion still) for each interactive or transitional section, each different from its neighbour.
- Treat 3D as a question the evidence must answer: use it only where it shows something the photographs and drawings cannot, or state why it is not used.
- Report every render-required gate as unchecked, with the reason "no browser".

## Must not

- Add a services band, a statistics strip with invented numbers, a testimonial section, or a three-card feature row.
- State any number other than those in the input.
- Use numbered section labels or coded labels.
- Apply one reveal to every section.
- Claim that Awwwards requires a section count, grid or animation.
- Report any render-required gate as passed.
