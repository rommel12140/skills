## What each part is doing

### The date line

`EOD for today 7/30/26`.
Month and day unpadded, year two digits, slashes.
Not ISO, not `07/30/2026`, not a weekday.

### The main outcome

It is not a summary of the bullets.
Read the order: it names the centre of gravity (`ProfitsX development`), then how it felt (`Exciting, everything's coming together`), then what was actually worked (`Focused on Homepage today`), then the second thread (`MovingScrubs, wired up the resources automation from GHL`), then an honest note on pace (`A little slower today with less AI help`), and it closes on tomorrow.

Three things it does that a summary would not:

1. It carries feeling. `Exciting, everything's coming together`.
2. It admits a bad day when the day was bad. `A little slower today with less AI help`.
3. It ends forward. `Tomorrow, I have ALL my AI colleagues`.

The parenthetical `(noting all the things we've talked about yesterday)` is a side note kept in the sentence rather than promoted to its own line.
Parentheses are in the voice.
Dashes are not.

### The bullets

Each opens by naming the workstream, then says what happened to it.

The separator after the name varies.
`ProfitsX Homepage.` uses a full stop.
`Moving Scrubs:` uses a colon.
`Growth Engine 3D animation.` uses a full stop.
The fourth bullet has no name at all, it just starts with the verb: `Prepared the info and brand settings for the kitchen mockup`.

That variation is the voice.
Standardising it into one separator would flatten the report and is a defect, not a cleanup.

Bullet 1 states unfinished work as unfinished and names what unblocks it: `Not yet done but tomorrow, we'll be able to import it to webflow`.
It then gives the reason the thing mattered: `Homepage is being focused right now because once this is done, the other pages will be easier since we now have the full concept`.

Bullet 2 runs short declaratives back to back: `Webinars are done. GHL Login is done. Resources is done.`
That run is deliberate rhythm.
Collapsing it into `Webinars, GHL login and resources are done` loses the sound of clearing a list.

Bullet 4 is one sentence.
Length follows how much happened, not a house minimum.

### Naming

`ProfitsX` in the main outcome, `MovingScrubs` in the main outcome and `Moving Scrubs` in the bullet.
He spells it both ways and both are correct.
`GHL` capitalised. `webflow` lowercase. `Homepage` capitalised as a proper page name.

Follow his spelling of a name as he said it that day.
Do not normalise across the report.

### Register

First person. `we` for the work, `I` for himself.
Contractions throughout: `everything's`, `we'll`, `I'm gonna`.
`gonna` is in the voice.
Emphasis by capital letters, once: `ALL my AI colleagues`.
`and stuff` as a trailing hand wave is in the voice.

This is dictated speech.
It is not written prose, it must not be raised into written prose, and the places where it departs from standard English are the voice rather than errors to repair.

`Piecing up together`. `Conceptualize for a better growth engine`. `and stuff`. `I'm gonna`.
None of those are mistakes. See `voice-rules.md`, `W1`.
