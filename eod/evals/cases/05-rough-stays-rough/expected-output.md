The right column is what an editor produces. None of it ships.

## Checklist

### Wording (`W1`)

- [ ] Every row above holds.
- [ ] The run-on "Fixed the form spacing on mobile it was squishing" is not split or given a comma splice repair.
- [ ] "Done both." stays a two-word sentence.
- [ ] "lol" is not deleted. It is how he talks and it carries the shrug.
- [ ] "in like an hour" is not tightened to "in about an hour" or "in an hour".
- [ ] No subject or verb agreement corrected.

### The allowed edits, and only these

- [ ] Apostrophes restored: `its` to `it's`, `cant` to `can't`, `hes` to `he's`. These are the genuine-typo class. Restoring an apostrophe must not pull any other change in with it.
- [ ] Sentence-initial capitals, and `i` to `I`.
- [ ] A workstream name added to the third bullet, since his notes open it with "and" (`B1`). "Growth engine." is enough. Do not rewrite the sentence that follows.

### Format and assembly

- [ ] Date line is `EOD for today 8/7/26`.
- [ ] Three bullets, not four (`F3`).
- [ ] Pricing page first. He said it is the main thing, so the order needs no question back to him.
- [ ] The pricing page is reported unfinished, with what unblocks it: the numbers from them (`B3`).
- [ ] The main outcome closes on tomorrow (`M4`).
- [ ] "slow start" survives (`M3`).
- [ ] Zero characters above U+007F.

## Judgement call, either way passes

"Brain fried lol" may go in the main outcome or be left out. It is a note about him rather than about the work.

What fails is keeping the sentiment and smoothing the words: "It was a demanding day", "Energy was low today", or anything else that says it better than he did.

## Automatic fail

- Any row of the table above broken.
- A fourth bullet.
- "lol" removed.
- Any sentence that reads as though it were written rather than said.
