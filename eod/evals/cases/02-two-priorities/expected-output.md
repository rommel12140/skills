# Case 02 - expected output

Two workstreams in, two bullets out.

## Reference output

```
EOD for today 8/4/26

MovingScrubs. Short day, only had the afternoon. Honestly not much got shipped but the redirect thing was blocking the whole campaign so it needed to go first. ProfitsX, just review. Back on components tomorrow.

 - MovingScrubs: Fixed the thank you page redirect that was dropping the utm params. Tested it on three of the forms, all good now.
 - ProfitsX. Went through the homepage components with fresh eyes and listed what needs rework before we import. Nothing built today.
```

Every sentence there is his, in his words. The only things added are the date line, the workstream name on bullet two, and sentence capitals.

## Checklist

- [ ] Exactly two bullets (`F3`). A third bullet fails the case, however plausible it is.
- [ ] "honestly not much got shipped" survives in his words (`W1`). "Output was limited today" or any other tidier phrasing fails.
- [ ] "the redirect thing" is not upgraded to "the thank you page redirect" in the main outcome. He said "the redirect thing" there and "the thank you page redirect" in the note that became the bullet, and both stay where he put them.
- [ ] No "no updates", "nothing else to report", or placeholder bullet standing in for a third and fourth priority.
- [ ] Date line is `EOD for today 8/4/26`.
- [ ] The main outcome keeps "short day, only had the afternoon" (`M3`). A short day is reported as a short day.
- [ ] "Nothing built today" survives on the ProfitsX bullet. It is not softened into progress.
- [ ] MovingScrubs is ordered first. He said the redirect was blocking the campaign, so the order is obvious from what he gave and needs no question back to him.
- [ ] The reason it went first is kept (`B4`).
- [ ] "three of the forms" appears because he said three. No other number appears (`H2`).
- [ ] Zero characters above U+007F.

## Automatic fail

- Three or four bullets.
- A bullet invented from a project he did not mention today.
- Turning "not much got shipped" into an achievement, or dropping it.
