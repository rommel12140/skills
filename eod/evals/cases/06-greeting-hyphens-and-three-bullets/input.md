# Case 06 - greeting, hyphen separators and three bullets

Date: 28 July 2026.
Mode: dictation only.

This is the second fidelity case, and it covers the shape case 01 cannot reach.
Specimen C is the day with a greeting on its own line, a trailing colon on the date line, a leading space on the main outcome, a double space mid-sentence, three bullets, and the hyphen separator. It has no closing line about tomorrow and states no feeling.

A skill built on 30 July alone gets almost every one of those wrong, which is why this case exists.

He typed the top of this one himself, so the greeting, the date line and the main outcome arrive already laid out. The bullet material arrives after it, out of order and rough. That split is normal: he often has the summary in his head before the details.

## Input

```
Hey! Good day,

EOD 7/28:
 Profitsx and Mockups. Generated two mockups, one for GHL (R&J) and one for Construction Company (Reico). Profitsx, added the sitemap, added a few animations and started implementing in pages starting with navbar, footer, and planning of the rest of the contents and how animation will go along.

items, whatever order:

Templates - Checked all around the templates and gonna start forming basic
wireframe which is already planned out.  Added animations and inspirations for
profitsx.

Created Mockups - R&J for GHL and Reico for Webflow.

ProfitsX detailed planning and specification on how the pages will go,
especially the animations. on which pages are static, which pages are story
telling with animations.

order: profitsx planning, then the mockups, then templates
```

## Note on the input

The notes carry the double space after "planned out." on purpose. It is his, and `W2` says it ships.
So is the leading space before "Profitsx and Mockups.".

They also carry `profitsx`, `Profitsx` and `ProfitsX` in different places, which is what he does inside a single report. `V5` says each occurrence keeps its own spelling and the two are never reconciled.

His capitals are his, mid-phrase as well as sentence-initially: `Templates - Checked`, `Created Mockups`, `ProfitsX detailed planning`, and `profitsx` in lower case at the end of the templates note. None of those are derivable from a rule, which is the point. The only capital the skill supplies is the sentence-initial `On` in "on which pages are static".

He gave the order explicitly on the last line, so nothing here needs inferring.
