# Why this skill reports no score

Awwwards publishes a jury scoring system.
This skill cites that system to explain what the award evaluates.
It does not impersonate the jury or convert a checklist into a prediction of winning.

A composite number hides which decision is weak and which evidence is missing.
A page can pass every anti-slop gate while remaining poorly composed or unconvincing.
It can also contain an intentional exception while serving its audience well.
Counting tells cannot establish authorship, originality, user satisfaction or award odds.

Report evidence, strengths, specific findings, fixes and readiness instead.
Counts by severity describe the review workload, not the design's quality.
Measured values such as contrast, LCP and target size remain useful when their conditions and meaning are stated.
Published award weights are research facts, not a local quality score.

When asked for a score, explain the limitation briefly and provide a criterion-by-criterion review.
Separate observed failures, design judgments, accepted exceptions and unchecked areas.
