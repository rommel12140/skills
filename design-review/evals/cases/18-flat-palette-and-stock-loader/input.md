# Flat palette and a stock loader

Mode: Refine.
A garden designer's site opens with a dark moss-green hero, cream type and a real fern photograph as its mark.
From the second section down, every chapter is cream type on one near-black ground, and in the light theme near-white on near-white.
The owner says everything below the hero is just white.
The page loads behind a plain progress line that fills left to right.

A previous candidate gave the three project chapters a purple, a blue and an orange ground and was rejected twice.

Synthetic supplied diagnostic evidence:

- The product screenshots on the page contain moss green, a lime highlight, a soft gold and a silver sage.
- The header keeps the hero's ground over every section.
- In the light theme the booking bars in the second chapter are cream on cream, ratio 1.3.
- The progress line is a separate div with its own stylesheet and no relation to the fern.
- The loader cuts to the hero with no handoff, and progress sometimes slides backwards when the exit tween starts.

Propose the colour and loading corrections and the evidence you would ship.
