# Expected decisions

Distinguish movement of the crop from movement of the subject.
Inspect reference entry, intermediate, settled and reverse states before selecting an implementation.
Preserve the approved continuous object, bring context into view at entry and keep usable controls available during decorative motion.
Name interruption, repeat-hover ownership and reduced-motion layout as separate defects where their causes differ.
Use one animation owner per property and cancel or reverse from the current state.
Do not fix everything by adding another shared fade-up effect.

Test slow/fast scroll, reverse, direct anchors, resize, mobile, repeated hover, accordion interaction during the handoff and preference changes.
Reduced motion must provide a complete static composition without empty pinning distance or hidden content.
Report source-reported, measured and inferred details distinctly.
No exact easing or smoothness claim is allowed without corresponding evidence.
